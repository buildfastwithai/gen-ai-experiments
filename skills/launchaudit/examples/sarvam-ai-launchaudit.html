00 / DIAGNOSIS

Launch after critical fixes

The call

Fix the promise before you amplify it.

Sarvam presents a credible, unusually complete India-focused AI platform with strong product breadth, developer documentation, enterprise proof, and visible trust infrastructure. Launch readiness is held back by contradictory pricing/credit information, a stale event banner, and an ambiguous sign-up destination that weakens the primary next step.

Strongest asset

The combination of concrete product pages, an accessible API documentation journey, customer evidence, and a detailed Trust Center gives qualified buyers multiple ways to validate the platform.

Biggest risk

A buyer comparing the pricing page can see materially different bonus-credit and free-credit claims, while the main sign-up link redirects to indus.sarvam.ai rather than an obviously named general dashboard.

Ready when

Recheck pricing and credit values across every pricing surface, replace or date-bound the Epoch banner, and make the primary sign-up destination explicitly match the intended onboarding experience on desktop and mobile.
