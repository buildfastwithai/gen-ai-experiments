01 / MARKET

Who this is for, and why now

Core problem

General-purpose AI and cloud APIs can be expensive, fragmented, or less reliable for Indian-language, code-mixed, speech, and document workflows.

Promised outcome

Ship Indian-language AI features faster with one API surface, local-language model specialization, transparent usage pricing, and support for production workloads.

Ideal customer

Indian BFSI, government-service, and customer-support software companies shipping multilingual document or voice automation

Buying trigger

a production launch, tender, contact-center localization project, KYC/document backlog, or failed generic-model accuracy test

Positioning

For India-focused product and operations teams that need multilingual voice or document automation, Sarvam AI is the Indian-language AI platform that combines specialized models, a unified API surface, and production-oriented support so teams can launch localized workflows without stitching together multiple providers.

Qualification signals
  • At least 10,000 monthly documents, minutes, or localized characters
  • Indian-language or code-mixed quality is a launch blocker
  • Existing cloud/API budget and named technical owner
  • Needs SLA, support, VPC, or on-premise discussion
Where to find them
  • BFSI and govtech product communities
  • India-focused developer and AI meetups
  • Cloud marketplace and systems-integrator partners
  • LinkedIn outreach to engineering/product leaders triggered by localization or automation launches
  • Sarvam docs, cookbook, and developer community

02 / MODEL

Build the business around one sharp wedge

Recommended model

Usage-based API credits with prepaid Pro/Business tiers and enterprise commitments; use paid pilots to prove recurring production volume.

Distribution wedge

Benchmark-led pilots for high-volume Indian-language document and voice workflows in BFSI/govtech/customer-support software.

North-star metric

Monthly gross profit from retained production accounts, with supporting measures of qualified pilots, paid conversion, usage volume, and support cost per account.

Go-to-market message

Evaluate your real Indian-language documents/audio against Sarvam and get to a production decision with transparent usage economics and a single integration surface.

14-day or fixed-credit production pilot with one workflow, acceptance metrics, solution-engineer review, and a conversion path to Pro/Business credits or enterprise terms

Risks to validate
  • Paid conversion from free credits is too low — Use fixed-credit pilots with a named production workflow and buyer acceptance metric.
  • Hyperscaler bundling or lower prices compress differentiation — Sell accuracy, code-mix performance, deployment fit, and workflow outcomes rather than API price alone.
  • Free usage creates uncapped inference/support cost — Keep free access credit-limited and rate-limited; charge for production support.
  • Enterprise pilots become services-heavy — Standardize pilot scope, acceptance tests, and paid implementation boundaries.
  • Model changes or quality gaps reduce trust — Version models, publish changelog guidance, and maintain customer regression datasets.
