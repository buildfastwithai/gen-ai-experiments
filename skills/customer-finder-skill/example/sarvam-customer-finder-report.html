02 / PATTERNS

Signals that repeat.

Pattern 01

Voice is becoming the access layer

Payments, commerce, banking, and public enquiry systems are moving beyond menus and typing toward voice-led navigation and task completion.

Pattern 02

Hindi/Hinglish is the current floor, not the finish line

Several organizations have live English/Hindi systems but public product pages point to broader regional-language coverage, code-mixing, or local-language accessibility as the next gap.

Pattern 03

Regulated, high-volume workflows are the strongest wedge

Banking and payments combine large repetitive volumes, authentication and audit requirements, and a clear business case for better language access.

Pattern 04

Incumbent AI creates expansion opportunities

Several prospects already have bots or voice experiences, so the realistic motion is a language-quality benchmark, new-language pilot, or complementary API—not a rip-and-replace sale.

Seven-day manual plan

Offer a narrowly scoped, manual evaluation or language-quality benchmark tied to the prospect’s public workflow; ask one discovery question before proposing a pilot.
