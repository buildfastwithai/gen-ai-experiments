Jev Post Scorer

Scores your X drafts against the mechanics in xAI's open-source ranking code.
The key is stored in chrome.storage.local on this machine only — it is not synced to your Google account and never touches the x.com page. Your draft text is sent from the extension's service worker to api.typesafe.ai to be scored, and nowhere else.

Scoring fires about 750 ms after you stop typing, only once the draft passes 20 characters, and identical text is served from cache rather than re-billed.
